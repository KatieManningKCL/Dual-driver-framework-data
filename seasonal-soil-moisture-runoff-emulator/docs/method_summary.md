# Method Summary

The emulator workflow predicts seasonal 1 km soil moisture and runoff-related variables for Great Britain under future climate scenarios.

1. Historical seasonal climate, hydrological target, PFT/land-use, soil and terrain datasets were harmonised to a 1 km Great Britain grid.
2. Multiple emulator model families were trained and compared using historical CHESS-land hydrological targets.
3. The final process-informed hybrid model was selected because it provided a stronger balance between predictive performance, hydrological consistency and plausible future climate response.
4. Future projections were generated for RCP2.6, RCP4.5 and RCP8.5 for 2020-2079.
5. Three validation and plausibility checks were performed: scenario consistency across RCPs, G2G validation for RCP8.5, and CDS/EURO-CORDEX hydrology consistency checks across RCP2.6, RCP4.5 and RCP8.5.

The validation supports the broad scenario ordering of the emulator outputs, especially stronger high-emission soil-moisture drying and runoff response under RCP8.5. Runoff validation is interpreted carefully because local grid-cell total runoff, G2G river flow and CDS river discharge are related but not identical variables.
